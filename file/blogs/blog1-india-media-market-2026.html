India's media and entertainment industry is no longer playing catch-up with the world. It's writing its own rules. With the sector crossing ₹2.78 trillion in 2025 and growing at nearly double the global average, digital is now the foundation — not the future.

PwC's Global Entertainment & Media Outlook 2026–30 projects India's E&M sector growing at 7.4% CAGR — from USD 25.7 billion in 2025 to USD 36.7 billion by 2030, nearly double the global rate of 4%. The FICCI-EY Media & Entertainment Report 2026 shows advertising growing 13.5% to INR 1.5 trillion, with digital commanding 63% of total ad revenues.

India's ad-to-GDP ratio still stands at just 0.41% — far below China (0.6%), UK (1.2%), and USA (1.4%) — signalling significant headroom for growth. Combined with Dentsu-E4M's projection of digital reaching ₹98,034 crore by 2027, the structural tailwinds for India's media market are unlike any other economy.

Trend 02 · Advertising

Digital Hits 63% of All Ad Spend — and It's Still Accelerating

₹49,000 crore in FY25 · 20% YoY growth · ₹71,621 crore (Dentsu) · 17% CAGR

India's advertising industry reached INR 1.5 trillion in 2025 (FICCI-EY), growing 13.5%. Within this, digital's dominance arrived years ahead of schedule: ET Brand Equity–Ipsos confirms digital ad spending hit ₹49,000 crore in FY2024–25, a 20% year-on-year jump, claiming 44% of total ad spend. Dentsu-E4M places the same market at ₹71,621 crore, projecting 17% CAGR to reach ₹98,034 crore by 2027 — equivalent to 70% of all advertising.

Mobile drives 78% of all digital ad spend, confirming the smartphone as the primary marketing screen. Social media (29.8%) and online video (29%) together account for nearly 60% of all digital formats.

Trend 03 · Social Platforms

Facebook & Instagram Dominate — But the Platform Mix Is Shifting Fast

$4.2B total digital ads · 8.6 trillion impressions · Instagram: 390M+ users

Sensor Tower's State of Digital Advertising in India 2026: India's digital ad impressions crossed 8.6 trillion in 2025 (+18% YoY). Facebook led platform spend at USD 1.88 billion, followed by Instagram at USD 1.70 billion, with YouTube at USD 0.37 billion. PwC confirms India is now Instagram's largest market globally with 390M+ users.

Generative AI sub-categories surged 204% in H2 2025 alone. October 2025 alone crossed USD 430 million in digital ad spend — driven by Diwali festive campaigns.

Trend 04 · OTT & Streaming

OTT Crosses 216 Million Paid Subscribers — Regional Languages Now Dominate

10.2% CAGR · USD 2.2B → USD 3.6B by 2030 · 56% regional language content

India's OTT video market is on a structural ascent. PwC projects the segment to grow from USD 2.2 billion in 2025 to USD 3.6 billion by 2030 at a 10.2% CAGR. FICCI-EY data shows digital subscriptions grew 60% in 2025 to INR 163 billion, with paid video reaching 143 million households.

Regional language content's OTT share leaped from 27% in 2020 to 56% in 2025. 200,000 hours of original content were produced in 2025, the majority in regional languages.

Music streaming crossed INR 59 billion with paid subscribers growing 37%. Even print grew 1% to INR 259 billion, with 39% of Indians still reading a physical newspaper.
